# Club editorial conventions

Write in Slovenian with the established warm club voice. Use supplied facts and match/roster records; preserve corrected spellings exactly. Photos alone do not establish identities, results or attendance. Do not invent an account of play from a score.

Use a concise factual title and excerpt; short paragraphs and headings when useful. A standard match report can cover the date/opponent/result, confirmed scorers and a user-supplied highlight. First-goal claims require confirmation. Article date and event date can differ: publishedAt is used to sort news; albums keep the event date. Keep explicit multi-day date labels.

Use originals from the matching local event folder for covers. A scorecard/club-logo image should use imageFit contain; event photos use the existing crop presentation. Do not add photo credits or Instagram source links unless requested. Link an available album through gallerySlug so the site renders its album button.

When correcting a match with linked news, inspect title/excerpt/body for scores and scorer names. The audit flags score discrepancies and missing expected scorer names; it does not prove every factual sentence. Update the prose from confirmed facts rather than blindly replacing every number.
