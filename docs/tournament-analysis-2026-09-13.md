# Tournament analysis — 13 September 2026

30 players · 11 preliminary rounds each · 55 preliminary matches. The supplied export matches the public database-backed API exactly (verified 14 September 2026).

## Method

For each focal player, remove every preliminary match they participated in, on either side, including any joker appearances. Calculate each other player’s average personal points per remaining confirmed preliminary match (excluding that other player’s extra joker appearances). These estimates are specific to the focal player.

For each regular appearance of the focal player, average the estimates of the three opponents and separately those of the other two teammates. Average each quantity across appearances, then subtract teammate support from opposition strength. Repeated opponents and teammates count each time. Higher differences indicate harder draws. Units are points per match, not final point totals, Elo, or bonus standings points. All playoffs are excluded.

If any encountered teammate or opponent has no remaining counted matches, the overall difficulty is unavailable rather than substituting zero or dropping that player. The minimum number of matches behind any encountered player estimate is reported. Small samples imply greater uncertainty. This method removes direct dependence on the focal player’s match scores, but remaining games still reflect varying teammates and opposition; it does not establish intrinsic ability or prove luck. Official points and ranking history are unchanged.
